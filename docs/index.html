Quote and Follow-up Desk

Quotes take too long to build by hand, and follow-ups slip. Deals go cold.

  • Claude reads rough call notes into what the business needs
  • Prices it with bundle, multi-line and term discounts, and a PDF quote
  • Caps any extra discount so the deal never drops below the margin floor
  • Follow-ups on day 2, 5 and 10 become Linear tasks, and stop when the client replies

Lead Radar

Reps lose hours deciding which businesses to call first.

  • Scores 200 businesses on fit and contract timing, and ranks the 178 that are not already customers
  • Shows exactly why each lead scored what it did
  • Claude drafts a first email for each top lead
  • Top leads land in Linear as tasks, with no duplicates on re-runs
Open the demo Code
Live demo

Account Watch

After the deal closes, onboarding steps get missed and renewals sneak up.

  • An onboarding checklist for every new account, built from the services it bought
  • A health score from 0 to 100 with the reason for every point lost, plus upsell signals
  • A daily CronJob raises one Linear issue per renewal at 90 days and escalates it at 60 and 30
  • A weekly Slack digest of renewals, at-risk accounts and overdue onboarding

How it's built

Kubernetes and HelmEvery tool runs on one cluster from one Helm chart, with Postgres and CronJobs for follow-ups, renewals and a weekly digest.
ClaudeReads call notes and drafts emails. Every tool still works in demo mode without a key.
LinearThe rep's to-do list inside the apps, and where the build itself is planned and tracked.
Python, Streamlit, GitHub ActionsTested on every pull request: unit tests, an app smoke test, chart validation and image builds.
